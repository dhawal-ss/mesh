Privacy notice

Mesh does not run your chosen account service

Draft reviewed: July 29, 2026. Owner/legal review is required before external beta publication.

Desktop app

Mesh stores the session and local message cache needed to operate the app on your device. Account sessions use the operating system's protected credential facilities where supported. Mesh does not silently contribute your disk, bandwidth, or battery to shared storage.

Account and community services

Your selected Matrix-compatible account service processes registration, identity, synchronization, encrypted event envelopes, and service metadata under its own privacy notice. Community room servers participating through federation also process Matrix metadata and encrypted events. Review provider disclosures before choosing.

Messages and media

Supported rooms use Matrix end-to-end encryption. Encryption does not hide all metadata, and provider retention or media behavior can vary. Mesh cannot promise deletion from backups, federated servers, other members' devices, or provider systems beyond supported APIs and policies.

Diagnostics

The public site uses no behavioral analytics. The desktop app does not upload message content for telemetry. Diagnostic logs remain local unless you deliberately copy and share them; secret-like values and local paths are redacted from user-facing diagnostic output.

Your choices

Mesh provides local export, logout, and account-deactivation controls. Remote deactivation and deletion depend on the selected service. Removing local state does not itself erase data retained by a provider or another participant.

Privacy questions and provider-specific requests should follow the routes on the support page.
