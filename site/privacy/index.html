Privacy notice

What Mesh stores, sends, and never uploads on its own

Product draft updated August 9, 2026. Owner and legal approval are required before public beta publication.

What Mesh stores on your device

Mesh stores your signed-in session, settings, recovery reminders, and the message cache needed to run the app. Protected account details use the operating system's secure storage where supported. Mesh does not use your device to host data for other people unless a future feature asks first and you choose to enable it.

What your chosen services receive

Your account provider carries the account and conversation data needed to sign in, keep conversations in sync, and deliver messages. A community may use a different service to carry its rooms. Each service has its own privacy notice, rules, retention, and support route. Mesh shows these choices separately and does not claim to operate independent services.

What encryption does and does not hide

Supported conversations protect message content between members' devices. Services can still see ordinary delivery details such as account addresses, room participation, timing, and data size. Mesh cannot promise that another member, a service backup, or an exported copy will delete information when you remove it from your device.

What Mesh never uploads automatically

Mesh does not automatically upload feedback, app errors, message content, recovery information, account addresses, room names, invitation links, or local file paths. Feedback and redacted support reports stay on your device until you choose to copy, save, or share them.

Your choices

You can choose an independent account provider, use another compatible service, sign out, remove local account data, and ask your provider about remote deactivation or deletion. Removing local data does not erase copies held by a provider or another participant.

Privacy questions and provider-specific requests should follow the routes on the support page.
