Third-party service disclosure

Choose where your account lives

Mesh is a client. Public account services and community-hosted services are operated independently and set their own rules.

Available paths

  • Matrix.org: a prominent independently operated public option.
  • Reviewed public services: a small checked-in catalog with operator, policy, registration, limit, source, and review metadata.
  • Community-hosted service: an optional account offer carried by an invitation, with no Mesh uptime guarantee.
  • Another service: any compatible homeserver selected by Matrix ID or service address.

What a choice means

The account service stores account and synchronization data and participates in encrypted conversations. It can observe normal Matrix metadata and encrypted event envelopes. Its terms, privacy policy, moderation, availability, registration, and limits apply.

The community's rooms may be hosted elsewhere. Federation lets an account from one compatible service join a room on another when room policy permits. A community registration token is offered only for that community's service and is never sent to a different provider.

No endorsement

Catalog inclusion is a dated review, not ownership, endorsement, permanence, or an SLA. Expired catalog entries are unavailable until re-reviewed. Mesh does not download or mirror raw public server directories at runtime and probes only the service a user selected.
